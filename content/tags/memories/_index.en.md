**To cherish, memories**

Because some stories should never be lost. Handwriting you would recognise among thousands. A scrap of fabric that brings back so many memories. Sometimes a whole life is hidden in something small.

With needle and thread, I give those memories a tangible form. A handwritten note becomes an embroidery, treasured garments become a comfort blanket, a small detail grows into something you want to keep forever.

Let yourself be inspired. Discover some of the creations I made before below. Every piece has its own story. Your story, your idea.
Perhaps you have a handwritten note, a special garment or a memory you would like to do something with, but you don’t quite know what yet.
You don’t need a finished plan. Together we can look for a form that suits your story.

Tell me your idea. I’m happy to think along with you.
