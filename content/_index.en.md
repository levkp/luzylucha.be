Welcome to Luz y Lucha, a quirky textile studio. Here, images take on a new meaning in hand-embroidered brooches,
bags and little treasures, clothes get a second life, and personal memories are turned into tangible creations such as
a birth blanket or a comfort blanket. It is a creative studio where art, craft and personal stories come together.
Sometimes playful, sometimes rebellious, sometimes moving, but always unique and with a story – a little piece of
Luz y Lucha.
